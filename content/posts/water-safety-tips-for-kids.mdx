Water safety isn't just a swim school topic — it's a life skill. Here are five habits every parent should instill early.

## 1. Never Swim Alone

Teach your child to always swim with a buddy or an adult present. Even strong swimmers can get into trouble — having someone nearby makes all the difference.

## 2. Enter Feet First in Unfamiliar Water

Jumping or diving into unknown water (lakes, reservoirs, beaches) is one of the leading causes of spinal injuries in children. Feet first, always.

## 3. Always Read and Follow Pool Safety Rules

Every pool has specific safety signs and rules posted for a reason. Before your child enters the water, take time to read them together and explain what they mean. Whether it's depth markers, lane rules, or diving restrictions, following these rules protects everyone in the pool.

## 4. Apply Sunscreen and Protect from Sun Exposure

In Singapore's tropical climate, UV rays are strong year-round, even on overcast days. Apply sunscreen (SPF 30+) 15 minutes before your child enters the water, and reapply every two hours or after swimming. Consider UV-protective clothing or rash guards for extended outdoor pool time.

## 5. Learn to Float

Floating on your back is the single most important survival skill in water. If your child can float and call for help, they have a fighting chance in an emergency. We teach this from day one at SwimKidsSG.
