We're thrilled to welcome you to SwimKidsSG — a swim school built around one goal: giving every child in Singapore the confidence and skills to be safe and happy in the water.

## What We Offer

Whether your child is stepping into the pool for the very first time or looking to refine their stroke technique, we have a programme designed for them:

- **Beginner** — water familiarisation, floating, and basic kicking
- **Intermediate** — freestyle and backstroke development
- **Advanced** — breaststroke, butterfly, turns, and endurance
- **Competitive** — dive starts, racing technique, refined turns, and competition training

## Small Classes, Big Results

We keep our class sizes small so every child gets personalised attention from a certified instructor. No child gets left behind — and no child gets bored waiting for their turn.
